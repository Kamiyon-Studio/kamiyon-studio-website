# Design System: Kamiyon Studio

## Overview

Last extracted from implementation: 12 November 2026.
The current public website uses sakura pink against warm charcoal, with ivory text and restrained rose-tinted surfaces.
This document records the existing identity and the approved frontend consistency work; it does not introduce new company claims or a replacement brand.

Shared headings, buttons, containers, and surfaces establish continuity across marketing pages and detail pages.
The homepage and interior opening heroes are static, so their content does not depend on scroll animation or video playback.
Other existing animation systems remain in use and retain reduced-motion behavior and their pause controls.

**Key Characteristics:**

- Sakura pink actions and emphasis, with charcoal text on pink fills.
- Geologica headings and controls paired with Montserrat supporting copy.
- Compact interior openings, readable text measures, and consistent container gutters.
- Real project content and imagery, with explicit empty or placeholder states where applicable.

Sources: `app/globals.css`, `app/themes/kamiyon.css`, `components/ui/Container.tsx`, `components/ui/Button.tsx`, `components/sections/HeroOpening.tsx`, `components/sections/PageOpeningHero.tsx`, and `lib/ui/display-heading.ts`.

Rendered verification is recorded separately by the orchestrator; this document makes no screenshot or deployment claim.
Older light-only statements in `context/ui-context.md` conflict with the current implementation and are preserved as historical context, not silently repaired here.

## Colors

Sakura is the primary brand accent; the lighter sakura ink serves text, focus, and hover states on dark surfaces.
Gold remains a secondary accent in existing components rather than a competing primary action color.
Charcoal is the page ground, ivory the main text, and rose-tinted secondary, accent, and card surfaces separate content without changing the palette.
Secondary and muted text use their dedicated tokens rather than arbitrary opacity.

**The Accent Contrast Rule.** Pink action fills use charcoal text; dark surfaces use ivory or the lighter sakura ink.

Text selection uses sakura with charcoal text.
Text carets use sakura, and the scrollbar pairs sakura with the page ground.

## Typography

Geologica supplies the display, heading, navigation, and control voice.
Montserrat supplies supporting paragraphs and form text.
Both are loaded through the existing Next.js font configuration.

The homepage has its own display scale; interior page titles and section titles use the shared scales in the frontmatter.
Interior titles balance lines and are limited to eighteen characters of measure, while lead copy is limited to sixty characters.
The shared section title has no decorative glow.

**The Shared Scale Rule.** Reuse the page and section heading treatments before introducing a route-specific size.

## Layout

The shared container is centered at a maximum width of eighty rem, with mobile, tablet, and desktop gutters recorded above.
Interior openings share top clearance of `clamp(7rem, 12vw, 10rem)`, three rem of bottom space, and a subtle bottom divider.
Supporting text sits below the title, and content sections continue in the same container alignment.

The homepage pairs a capabilities-led heading with real published project evidence in two columns, stacking them on smaller screens.
The project panel uses ivory with charcoal text and preserves the full composition of available project artwork.
When a real project has no artwork, its actual positioning and gameplay details provide the evidence instead.
Services use divided title-and-capability rows, stacked on mobile, without decorative placeholder images.
Portfolio cards and article lists preserve their distinct content formats instead of forcing every page into an identical card layout.

## Elevation & Depth

The updated project cards use a tinted surface and border without a hover shadow or tilt.
Service rows use a single dividing border, without a surrounding card or shadow.
Other legacy components still consume the global small, medium, and large shadow tokens.
This is a restrained shared treatment, not a claim that every component is shadow-free.

## Shapes

Buttons, form fields, and cards use gently rounded sixteen-pixel corners.
Pill shapes remain for compact badges and existing navigation actions.
Service rows use open spacing and a stable two-column layout rather than clipped copy panels.

## Components

### Buttons

The shared `Button` renders a native button or an appropriate link, without decorative WebGL work.
Primary actions use sakura; secondary actions use an outlined transparent surface; ghost actions use sakura ink.
All variants have a minimum default height of three rem, with small and large sizes supported.
Hover transitions change background and border colors; reduced motion disables those transitions.
Disabled buttons and links are visibly dimmed and cannot activate.

### Cards and lists

Project cards lead with an image or a typographic project-title fallback and retain project metadata and case-study links.
Service rows pair the existing title with its description, up to three capabilities, and the service link.
Capabilities use the existing gold accent for secondary emphasis.
Blog entries use a divided list with dates rather than decorative card wrappers.

### Inputs and badges

Contact fields use card surfaces, shared rounded corners, a rose border, and visible focus outlines.
Form pending, validation, error, and success behavior remains functional rather than encoded only by color.
Badges retain their distinction between informational labels and explicit placeholders.

### Navigation

The existing logo and menu trigger open a full-screen navigation dialog.
Its route links, service submenu, keyboard containment, Escape handling, and focus restoration remain unchanged by this visual pass.
Navigation uses the brand display font and sakura interaction treatments.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do reuse semantic color tokens, the shared container, and shared heading scales.
- Do keep controls readable and keyboard-operable in loading, disabled, and error states.
- Do preserve actual CMS content, link destinations, and truthful empty states.
- Don't reintroduce parallax or autoplay video into the opening heroes.
- Don't use white text on the primary pink action fill.
- Don't treat legacy light-only documentation as evidence that the current site has a theme switcher.
